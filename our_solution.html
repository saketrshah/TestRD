Thermoelectric Heat Recovery Unit - A Detailed Look

At the heart of every ΩΣnergy system is our Thermoelectric Heat Recovery Unit—a compact thermoelectric generator designed to capture waste heat from your home's various appliances.

This unit converts thermal energy that would otherwise be lost into usable electricity, feeding it back into your home's power supply. Typically installed in the garage or utility room, it operates silently and requires minimal maintenance.

Key features:

  • Compact design fits standard garage installations
  • Captures heat from multiple household appliances
  • Produces up to 10 kWh of electricity every day
